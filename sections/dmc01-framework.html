Project shape · 12-month typical

Weeks at the bookends, months in the deep work.

A typical engagement spends days at the start aligning on scope, weeks framing the problem, then months on assessment and execution. Delivery and handover close the loop in days.

Step 1 · Learn

A bespoke Statement of Work, signed and locked.

First contact converts to a paper trail: scope, deliverables, commercials and IP, agreed by both sides. Clean foundations make the rest of the project possible.

Step 2 · Think

Interview the room. Frame the problem.

Working sessions with each stakeholder — Sales, Product, Data, Legal — surface the real constraints. Their inputs converge into a single problem statement and a coordinated plan.

Step 3 · Build

Data-driven analysis, documented in Notion.

Findings, gaps and prioritised recommendations live in a single Notion workspace — one source of truth for the assessment, with every claim backed by data and traceable to a stakeholder input.

Step 4 · Execute

Figma for the design. VS Code for the plan.

Prototypes, pricing screens and pitch frames are designed in Figma; the data-led roadmap, model specs and SQL live in VS Code. The customer team gets both, in versionable form.

Step 5 · Deliver

An executive brief, ready for the board.

A polished close-out — strategy, projections, playbooks and templates handed over so internal teams can run the next chapter without you in the room.
