Framework

How the work actually gets done.

A structured approach from initial framing through to execution. Short cycles at the edges, deeper work where alignment is built and decisions stick.

  1. 01
    Learn ~ 2 weeks

    Framing & Alignment

    Align stakeholders, define the real problem, set a direction that holds. Formalised by the approval of a bespoke Statement of Work and execution of contractual terms.

  2. 02
    Think 2–4 weeks

    Design & Coordination

    Build the plan across commercial, product and delivery so everything moves together.

  3. 03
    Build 1–4 months

    Diagnosis & Direction

    Identify what matters, prioritise actions, and establish a single source of truth.

  4. 04
    Execute 1–6 months

    Execution & Iteration

    Work with teams to implement, test and refine until it works in practice.

  5. 05
    Deliver ~ 2 weeks

    Embedding & Handover

    Transfer ownership so the model runs in production.
