01Scope

This policy explains how DMC01 Ltd uses cookies and similar technologies on dmc01.com.

02What are cookies?

Small text files placed on your device that help the site function, remember actions and preferences, or help us understand how the site is being used. We also use privacy-respecting analytics where enabled.

03How we use cookies

  • Strictly necessary (always on) — site security, load balancing, form operation.
  • Analytics (optional) — to understand site performance and improve content.
  • Functional / embeds (optional) — content from third parties (e.g. Vimeo, Calendly) which may set cookies when you interact with them.

04Your choices (consent)

On your first visit, we show a banner asking for your consent for non-essential cookies. You can Accept all, Reject non-essential, or Manage preferences. You can change your preference at any time via "Cookie settings" in the footer.

06Managing cookies in your browser

You can block or delete cookies via your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will impact site functionality (forms may not submit, sessions may not persist).
