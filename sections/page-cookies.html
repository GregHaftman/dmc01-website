01Scope

This policy explains how DMC01 Ltd uses cookies and similar technologies on dmc01.com.

02What are cookies?

Small text files placed on your device that help the site function, remember actions and preferences, or help us understand how the site is being used. We use analytics cookies only if you allow them.

03How we use cookies

  • Strictly necessary (always on) — site security, spam protection on the contact form, and remembering your cookie choice.
  • Analytics (optional, off until you allow it) — Google Analytics, to understand how the site is used and improve it.

04Your choices (consent)

On your first visit, a banner asks whether you allow analytics cookies. You can Allow all, Reject all, or Customise. No optional cookie is set until you choose.

You can change your mind at any time via Cookie settings in the footer of every page. Withdrawing consent removes the analytics cookies.

We keep a record of each choice as evidence of consent: a random ID (also stored in your dmc01_consent cookie), your choice, the date and the page. It contains no IP address or other identifying information, and is deleted automatically after two years.

06Managing cookies in your browser

You can block or delete cookies via your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will impact site functionality (forms may not submit, sessions may not persist).
