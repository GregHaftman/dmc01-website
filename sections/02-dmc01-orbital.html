Clicking the logo does trigger something. Here's what's actually going on across the orbital:

Each glowing dot drifting between modules is one interaction weight — a moment where two modules influence each other in real engagements. The denser the dot traffic between two modules, the more they tend to iterate together in practice.

  • The more we iterate on Monetisation Model & Pricing, the more we have to retrain the Sales team — so dots flow heavily from there into Value Strategy & Enablement.
  • Offer Design & Packaging usually precedes the pricing engine, which is why those two share a constant back-and-forth.
  • And the pricing engine itself is built on the back of the licensing terms and workflow rights agreed upstream — so dots cascade in from Rights & Licensing.

It's not random. It's the choreography of how the modules actually fit together.
